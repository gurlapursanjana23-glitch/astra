What is IEEE?

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. Comprising more than 420,000 members in over 160 countries, IEEE fosters technological innovation through world-renowned publications, international conferences, consensus standards, and educational initiatives.

Branch Vision

To establish IEEE RRCE as a preeminent hub of engineering innovation, research mindset, and visionary student leadership.

Branch Mission

Equip students with practical engineering competencies, ethical leadership, and global industry networking through specialized technical societies.

Bangalore Section & RRCE

The IEEE Bangalore Section is recognized as one of the most vibrant sections in IEEE Region 10 (Asia-Pacific). Located in India's silicon capital, it provides students with direct access to Tier-1 technology companies and research institutions.

Established at RajaRajeswari College of Engineering, our Student Branch empowers undergraduates across all engineering disciplines through sponsored symposiums, paper publications, and technical mentorship.

Affinity Group

Women in Engineering (WIE)

Global network dedicated to promoting women engineers and scientists, inspiring students to follow technical and leadership interests in STEM.

  • Mentorship Circles with Women Tech Leaders
  • Annual WIE Leadership Summit & Talks
  • STEM Outreach & Technical Bootcamps
Technical Society Chapter

Robotics & Automation Society (RAS)

Fosters practical robotics, autonomous rovers, multi-rotor aerial drones, manipulator arms, and mechatronic control architectures.

  • ROS 2 & Computer Vision Hardware Labs
  • Autonomous Navigation & Maze Tracking Cups
  • Drone Flight Telemetry & SLAM Sessions
Technical Community

Signal Processing Society (SPS)

Focuses on transforming digital signals, speech synthesis, computer vision, biomedical signal analysis, and sensor fusion methodologies.

  • Digital Audio & Speech Synthesis Labs
  • Medical Image Segmentation Masterclasses
  • Radar & Telemetry Signal Processing
Technical Community

Industry Applications Society (IAS)

Bridges electrical systems, power electronics, renewable micro-grids, embedded industrial controllers, and IoT telemetry for manufacturing.
